Plantation Activities

Planting

Pruning
Removing unwanted branches to help plants grow well.
Harvesting
Picking ripe coffee cherries from the trees.
Drying
Removing moisture from coffee beans.
Packaging
Packing dried coffee beans for storage or sale.
